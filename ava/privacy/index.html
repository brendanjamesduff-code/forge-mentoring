Google Calendar and your data

Ava Privacy Notice

Last updated 9 November 2026

Who we are

Forge Mentoring Ltd is responsible for the way Ava handles personal information. Ava is the internal assistant Forge uses to manage its schedule and coordinate work. For privacy questions or requests, email brendan@forgementoring.co.uk.

What Ava accesses and why

When an account holder connects Google Calendar, Ava can read, create and delete events on calendars where the connected account has the relevant permissions. Forge uses Ava to check upcoming commitments, schedule or remove events, and prepare for meetings.

Depending on the event and the task, Ava may use:

  • Event titles, start and end dates or times, time zones and whether an event lasts all day.
  • Calendar and event identifiers to identify the correct event.
  • Locations or video meeting links when meeting details are needed.
  • Organiser or attendee names and email addresses when working with invitations.
  • Event descriptions or notes when reviewing meeting details or preparing a briefing.

Ava retrieves event information from Google Calendar. When creating an event, Ava sends the relevant event details to Google Calendar. When deleting an event, Ava uses the calendar and event identifiers to tell Google which event to remove.

Our lawful basis

Forge Mentoring relies on legitimate interests to use personal information in Calendar events. Our legitimate interest is managing Forge’s appointments and coordinating its work.

Where Ava processes Calendar information

Ava processes and stores Calendar information on Forge Mentoring’s own servers. Access within Forge is restricted to its directors and is subject to Google’s Limited Use requirements. Ava exchanges event information with Google Calendar to provide the integration. Forge does not send Calendar information to OpenAI or other cloud services for processing or storage.

Forge’s use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will comply with the Google Workspace user data and developer policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How long Forge keeps Calendar information

Forge keeps personal information from Calendar events for as long as it is needed to manage appointments and coordinate work. For client-related information, Forge keeps it while the client is active and the information is still needed for that purpose. When the client relationship ends or the information is no longer needed, whichever comes first, Forge deletes or anonymises the copies it holds.

Disconnecting Google Calendar and requesting deletion

You can revoke Ava’s Calendar access in the connections section of your Google Account. This stops future access through that authorisation. To ask Forge to delete information it already holds, email brendan@forgementoring.co.uk.

Your right to object

You can object at any time to Forge using your personal information on the basis of legitimate interests. To object, email brendan@forgementoring.co.uk.

Your other rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may also have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information or restrict how Forge uses it. You can raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.
