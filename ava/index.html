How Forge works

Meet Ava, the AI behind Forge’s day-to-day work

Ava connects the tools we use to plan our work, prepare for meetings and follow through on decisions.

Ava Privacy Notice

Ava is the AI assistant we use every day at Forge Mentoring. She helps us plan the week, prepare for meetings, find the information we need and turn conversations into drafts and next steps. When several projects are moving at once, Ava helps us keep track of what we’ve agreed and what needs our attention next.

How Ava fits into Forge

Ava is our day-to-day starting point. We can ask her what’s coming up, talk through a piece of work, or give her a rough idea and use the result as a first draft. She works alongside other AI agents in the Forge team, including Jarvis and Alfred, Gareth’s AI agent. Our approach is AI-assisted and human-led. The Forge team decides what to act on and takes responsibility for the work that goes out.

How Ava connects to our tools

Ava connects a range of local systems we run ourselves and cloud services such as Google Workspace. She uses APIs to connect these tools, so we can work with information from several places in one conversation.

Before a meeting, we can ask Ava what’s on the calendar, pull in relevant notes from our own systems and prepare a briefing for the team to review. After the meeting, we can use the same conversation to turn our notes into a follow-up draft. That saves us having to gather the same context each time we switch tools.

Why Ava asks for Google Calendar access

Ava can read, create and delete events in Google Calendar through a connected account, subject to that account’s permissions. We use the connection to check upcoming commitments, schedule new events and remove events from the calendar. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the connections page in your Google Account.

Ava is an internal Forge Mentoring assistant. This page explains the app and its Google Calendar connection. For details on how Ava handles Google user data, see the Ava Privacy Notice.
