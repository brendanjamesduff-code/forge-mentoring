Using Ava

Ava Terms of Service

Terms for using Forge Mentoring’s internal assistant and its Google Calendar connection.

Last updated 9 November 2026

Who can use Ava

Ava is the internal assistant operated by Forge Mentoring Ltd. These terms apply to people Forge authorises to use Ava for its work.

Keep your sign-in details secure. Only connect Google accounts and calendars that you have permission to use for Forge work.

Using Google Calendar

When you connect Google Calendar, Ava can read, create and delete events within the permissions you grant. Check the calendar, date, time and guests before asking Ava to create an event. Check that you have identified the correct event before asking Ava to delete it.

Ava can make mistakes. Check important dates, event changes and drafts against the original source before relying on them or sharing them.

You can revoke Ava’s Calendar access through your Google Account connections.

Using Ava responsibly

Use Ava only for work you are authorised to carry out. Share information only when it is needed for the task and you have permission to use it. Respect any confidentiality obligations that apply.

Keep passwords, access tokens and other credentials out of prompts. If you suspect unauthorised access or notice an unexpected Calendar change, report it to Forge.

Privacy and access

The Ava Privacy Notice explains how Forge handles Google Calendar information and how you can make a privacy request.

Forge may restrict or remove access if your authorisation ends, if Ava is misused, or if needed to address a security issue.

Contact Forge

For questions about these terms or help with Ava, email brendan@forgementoring.co.uk.
