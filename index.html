About Forge

Calm structure for difficult organisational moments.

Forge is the working name of Forge Mentoring Ltd, a specialist consultancy focused on structured review, organisational insight, decision making, resilience, and practical change.

Forge helps organisations slow difficult situations down, understand the wider picture, identify risk, and move forward with clarity.

The work is led by Brendan Duff, whose background includes facilitation, mentoring, communication, training, public service, operational decision making, crisis communication, and team development.

Where the scope of the work requires additional support, Forge can work with trusted professional associates.

Who Forge supports

Organisations, leaders, and teams.

Forge can support charities, public bodies, private companies, community organisations, leadership teams, and operational teams.

The work may be useful after a difficult incident, internal conflict, workplace pressure, communication breakdown, repeated mistakes, leadership challenge, cultural concern, change, transition, risk exposure, or a need for independent review.

✓

Confidentiality is central to the work.

Forge operates with clear boundaries, discretion, and respect. Where appropriate, reviews can operate under Chatham House style principles. The insight can leave the room. The names do not.
