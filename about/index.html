This is Forge

Understanding the organisation behind the issue.

Forge is the working name of Forge Mentoring Ltd, a specialist consultancy focused on structured review, organisational insight, risk analysis, decision making, resilience, and practical change.

Forge supports organisations after incidents, challenges, conflict, operational pressure, communication breakdowns, leadership strain, or periods of uncertainty.

The purpose is not to arrive with a pre-written answer. The purpose is to create the structure that allows people to understand what happened, what it reveals, and what needs to change.

The Forge position

No judgement. No bias. Action on data.

Difficult organisational moments need calm, careful structure. Forge helps leaders and teams move from pressure to understanding, and from understanding to practical action.

The work is grounded in evidence, communication, human dynamics, risk, and organisational insight.

Led by Brendan Duff

Communicator first. Structure under pressure.

Brendan Duff leads Forge.

Brendan is a communicator first.

His work is built around the ability to enter complex situations, listen carefully, read the room, understand pressure, and create the structure people need to think clearly.

He brings together risk assessment, structured review, facilitation, project management, coaching, mentoring, training, stakeholder engagement, and practical organisational support.

His background includes operational service in demanding environments, including military deployment to Bosnia with the Princess Patricia’s Canadian Light Infantry. That experience sat within a wider peacekeeping environment focused on stability, self-sustaining peace, governance support, humanitarian assistance, information gathering, and the protection of fragile communities.

That experience matters to Forge.

It means Brendan understands pressure beyond theory. He has worked in environments where information is incomplete, trust is fragile, communication matters, and decisions carry consequence.

He is used to working with people, teams, leaders, and stakeholders when situations are unclear, sensitive, or difficult.

Brendan brings calm structure, careful listening, operational judgement, and practical challenge. He helps organisations understand what happened, what it reveals, and what needs to change.

His strength is the ability to read people, pressure, risk, culture, communication, and decision making in real time, then turn that understanding into something useful.

This is how Brendan Duff arrives: no judgement, no bias, just action on data.

Why Forge exists

Difficult moments reveal how organisations really work.

They show the strength of communication, decision making, leadership, culture, trust, and resilience. They also reveal the gaps that are easy to miss when everything appears normal.

Pressure

Pressure exposes reality.

When pressure rises, organisations often discover where roles are unclear, communication is weak, decisions are delayed, or people are carrying more than they can say.

Risk

Risk is not always obvious.

Risk can sit beneath the surface in assumptions, culture, silence, fatigue, unresolved conflict, unclear accountability, or repeated patterns that have become normal.

Change

Insight should lead somewhere.

A review only matters if it helps the organisation act. Forge turns insight into practical recommendations, clearer decision making, and meaningful next steps.

The Forge approach

Clear, human, and practical.

Forge works with the reality of people, pressure, risk, communication, and culture.

The work is calm and structured. It does not rely on jargon, theatre, or a generic consultancy script. It is built around listening properly, asking useful questions, identifying risk, and helping organisations act with clarity.
